06

So what

Three signals for gaming, and what we could build

1

A launch is now a live broadcast

Up to 7.9M people were watching at one moment. Launches will be planned around creators and time zones, not just stores.

We could build a live launch dashboard: viewers, sentiment, reviews and outages in one view.

2

Simulation is the new bottleneck

Rockstar chose a denser, reactive world over frame rate. The next leap is smarter crowds, traffic and NPCs.

We could build crowd and NPC behaviour models, extending our player-movement work.

3

"Made by people" is a premium

Around 4,700 people built it, and its publisher says generative AI played no part. AI tools promise to close that cost gap for everyone else.

We could build critic intelligence that turns 150 reviews into insight within an hour of the embargo lifting.
